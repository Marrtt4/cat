Nine lives, five chapters

What a cat needs changes with each season. We keep the house ready for all of them.

0–6 months

Kitten

Four meals a day, soft landings, and a small room to learn the world.

6–24 months

Junior

Peak mischief. Vertical space, puzzle feeders, and patience for 3 a.m. sprints.

2–6 years

Prime

Sleek, confident, settled. Annual exams and dental care keep them here.

7–10 years

Mature

Lower steps, warmer beds, and a diet tuned for her kidneys and joints.

11+ years

Sovereign

Twice-yearly checkups, gentle routines, and more lap time than ever.

Daily rituals

Feed

Measured portions of high-protein, moisture-rich food, served at room temperature in a wide, shallow dish so whiskers stay untouched.

Play

Two short hunting sessions a day, stalk, chase, catch, then a meal. It mirrors their natural rhythm and ends the night-time zoomies.

Rest

A quiet, elevated place by a window. Cats sleep where they feel safe and can see, so give them the view and then leave them be.

Yes, dogs are welcome too.

They may visit, sit politely, and admire the furniture from a respectful distance. The house, however, remains the cat’s.
